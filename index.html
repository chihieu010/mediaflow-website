CREATOR WORKSPACE / MEDIAFLOW STUDIO

Your video workflow, in one place.

MediaFlow Studio is a locally hosted workspace for creators to organize video assets, render edits and publish to their own YouTube channels.

01 / ORGANIZE

A library for each channel

Keep video metadata, source references and channel assets together. Track each video throughout your workflow.

02 / CREATE

Render with your settings

Apply saved video and thumbnail configurations, channel logos and frames. Preview changes before creating an output.

03 / PUBLISH

Publish with permission

Connect a YouTube channel using Google OAuth. Upload your selected video, title, description and thumbnail, or enable a channel schedule.

You stay in control

The management application runs on your own Windows computer or configured server. This public website provides product information only; it does not connect to your channel or collect your Google credentials.

Optional Gemini or OpenAI integrations assist with titles and descriptions when configured by the operator. Only use media you own or are authorized to use.
