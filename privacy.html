Privacy policy

This policy explains how MediaFlow Studio handles information when you use its locally hosted application and this informational website.

Information accessed and stored

When you authorize Google OAuth, the application stores access and refresh tokens on the machine hosting the application. Depending on the permissions granted and APIs enabled, it accesses channel identifiers, channel statistics, authorized analytics, video metadata and upload information. Local records also include video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, settings, schedules and task logs.

How information is used

Information supports channel management, authorized statistics, rendering, thumbnail preparation and video publication requested by the operator or an enabled schedule. OAuth tokens are used to call Google APIs on behalf of the connected channel. MediaFlow Studio does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Optional AI services and other providers

If the operator enables Gemini or OpenAI, source titles, descriptions, supplied context and prompts are sent to that chosen provider to generate replacement text. Review that provider's terms and data settings before enabling it; do not include confidential information in prompts. OAuth tokens and browser cookies are not included in those AI prompts. Uploading sends the chosen video, thumbnail and metadata to YouTube. An operator-configured upload proxy may carry upload traffic.

Storage and security

Application data resides on the operator's configured host and storage locations. Saved OAuth tokens and API keys are encrypted at rest by the application. The operator is responsible for device security, access restrictions and backups. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Retention, deletion and revoking access

Locally stored media and records remain until removed by the operator. Moving a video to the application trash is not permanent deletion; use permanent deletion to remove its managed files. Local deletion does not delete a video already published on YouTube. To withdraw Google authorization, visit Google account permissions and remove MediaFlow Studio. Revocation alone does not erase existing local records or backups. For removal of credentials and other retained records from a managed installation, contact the operator at the address below; deletion requests will be handled within 30 days. Independently maintained backups must also be addressed by the host operator.

Limited Use

MediaFlow Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to train generalized AI or machine learning models by MediaFlow Studio.

This website

This informational website has no login, advertising, analytics script or application database. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process request information under the GitHub privacy statement. Google services are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
